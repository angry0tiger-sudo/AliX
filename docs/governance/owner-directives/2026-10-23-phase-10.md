- Freqtrade 2026.7 trade schema is checked before reading its `trades` table. `close_profit_abs` is labelled as source-reported and is not account-reconciled.
- Phase 9 simulated Decision Ledger remains `SYNTHETIC_ONLY / NOT_RECONCILED`.
- Walk-forward reports sample size, win rate, net expectancy, maximum drawdown and explicit evaluation of the 60% win-rate / 15% maximum drawdown / positive-expectancy thresholds after the minimum 100-trade sample. Insufficient sample is NO-GO/INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE; passing metrics never promotes a Champion.
